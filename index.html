Privacy & your data

What Stepzo does with your data, and how to delete your account.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Privacy policy

The short version

  • Stepzo works without an account. Until you sign in, your routines stay on your phone.
  • If you create an account, we store your routines and history so they sync between your devices.
  • There are no ads, and we never sell your data.
  • You can export or delete everything whenever you like.

Who we are

Stepzo is an app from Digital Matty (“we”, “us”). We decide how the data described on this page is used. If you have a question about your data, email services@digitalmatty.com.

What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. Almost all of it exists only if you create an account.

DataWhat it includesWhy we need it
Account Email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), account ID and sign-up date. To sign you in and keep your account secure.
Profile Optional display name, avatar emoji and time zone. To show you in focus rooms and to time things for where you live.
Routines Routine names, icons, colours and reminder times; step titles and durations. To back up your routines and sync them between your devices.
History When you started and finished a routine, how many steps you completed or skipped, and extra time you added. To show your progress and weekly summary.
Settings Your sound, vibration, reminder and notification preferences. To keep the app set up the same way on each device.
Focus rooms Rooms you follow or create, when you join and leave a session, and reports you send about other people. To run focus rooms and keep them safe.
Purchases Whether Pro is active, which product you bought, when it expires and the App Store or Google Play reference for that purchase. We never receive your card details. To unlock the features you paid for.
Device Whether you use iOS or Android, a push notification token, the app version and when the app was last active. To send the notifications you have turned on.
Diagnostics Crash reports and anonymous usage events such as “routine started”. They never contain routine names or anything you type, and they are not linked to your account. To fix bugs and improve the app. You can turn usage data off.

We don’t ask about your health or any diagnosis, and we don’t collect your contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your phone

You can build routines, run the timer, set reminders and see your progress without signing in. In that case your data is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself.

When you sign out, you choose whether to keep your routines on the phone or remove them.

Who we share data with

We don’t sell your data and we don’t share it for advertising. We share it only with the services that help us run Stepzo, including:

Supabase
Hosts our database, sign-in and servers. Holds all of your account data.
Email delivery
Sends account emails such as password resets. Receives your email address.

Other people in focus rooms. Focus rooms have no chat. People in the same room can see your display name, your avatar emoji, a preset status and any reactions you send. They can’t see your routines, your history or your email address.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it, or when it is needed to protect the safety of our users.

How long we keep it

Account data
Until you delete your account.
Deleted routines and steps
They stay marked as deleted for up to 90 days so your other devices can sync the change. After that they are erased.
Focus room sessions
Erased about 30 days after they end, unless they are part of your own history.
Abuse-prevention counters
Erased within one day.
Backups
Overwritten within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy. In the app, go to Settings › Manage account › Export my data. You get everything as one file.
  • Correct it. Edit your routines and display name in the app at any time.
  • Delete it. See Delete your account below.
  • Turn off usage data. Go to Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data.
  • Control notifications. Change them in Settings, or in your phone’s own settings.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or limit how we use your data, to move it to another service, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email services@digitalmatty.com. We reply within 30 days.

We use your data to provide the service you asked for, to keep it secure and to improve it. Where the law requires your consent, we ask for it first.

How we protect it

  • Data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections.
  • Each account can read only its own data. The database itself enforces this.
  • Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • The keys for our providers are kept on our servers, never inside the app.

No system is completely secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Stepzo is made for adults. It is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Where your data is processed

Our database is hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in Singapore. Some of the providers listed above process data in other countries, including the United States. When data moves between countries, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer in their data processing terms.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also let you know in the app or by email.

Delete your Stepzo account

You can ask us to delete your Stepzo account, and the data linked to it, at any time. Stepzo is published by Digital Matty.

How to request deletion

Send us an email. You don’t need to have the app installed.

  1. Email services@digitalmatty.com from the address you signed up with.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Stepzo account.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request really comes from you.

Deletion can’t be undone.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted for good

  • Your account: email address and password
  • Your profile: display name and avatar
  • All routines, steps and reminder times
  • Your history and progress
  • Your settings
  • Your devices and push notification tokens
  • Focus room activity, private rooms you created and reports you sent

Kept for a limited time

  • Backups: deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, until they are overwritten.
  • Server logs: technical logs can contain your account ID. They expire on their own within 90 days.
  • Purchase records: Apple or Google keep the receipts for purchases made through their stores, under their own policies.
  • Anonymous data: usage statistics and crash reports that were never linked to your account.

Routines saved on your phone stay there until you remove them or uninstall the app.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so cancel there as well:

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play › profile picture › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions.
